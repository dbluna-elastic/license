Why this matters now (H1)

TDLR is in the Sunset Advisory Commission review cycle, and Texas DIR is pushing
statewide digital-government and shared-identity work (Texas.gov / TxT). The
political question in the room is no longer "can we digitize another PDF" — it is
whether a citizen still has to retype identity data the state already holds every
time they touch a new license program. This demo measures that waste at the field
level and watches the next published form so the finding does not go stale after
Sunset testimony.

Verify the current Sunset timeline and any DIR identity memo before quoting dates
to TDLR — a stale citation costs more than no citation.
